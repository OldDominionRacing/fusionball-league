Founding teams · Colorado Springs 2027

Field a team

The 2027 Invitational is the first county competition in Fusionball. Every team that enters is a founding team, and every member of it is an owner. Rugby clubs, flag football squads, unit teams, college clubs, and groups of friends who can put fifteen players and a coach on a field are all welcome.

Register interest

What a team is

  • 15 players and one non-playing coach. Eleven on the field at a time. Every player runs, throws, catches, and kicks.
  • 16 ownership units, one per person. One person, one unit, one vote. Nobody outside the team can own a piece of it.
  • The coach is the anchor. Rosters turn over; the coach holds the team's memory, its sponsors, and its playbook.
  • Military members keep their unit through deployment, and carry a portable unit right to a new team when they PCS.
  • All players 18 and over. Signed waivers; athletic trainer or EMT on site every game day.

What it costs, and what comes back

Founding members pay a buy-in set at cost recovery only: the team's sanction fee, player registration (which covers accident insurance), and kit. Under the current year-one model that works out to roughly $200 to $300 per member; the exact number is published before registration opens.

League surplus above published operating costs goes back to teams by finish position. On top of that, every team gets a sales code: a share of every ticket, season pass, and merchandise item sold through it goes straight to the team's treasury, whatever its finish.

This is a season of a new sport with equity attached, not an investment. Founding units are the cheapest they will ever be, if the sport grows. Read it as a bet, not a return.

Winter 2026–27Target hoops fabricated. Insurance bound. Presenting sponsor. Ticketing live.
Early 2027Self-officiated preseason pickup on city practice fields: touch or wrap-tackle, league-set fixtures, team-chosen locations. The freestanding hoop lets teams practice anywhere.
2027 seasonFour Saturdays at Ragain Field, 6303 Barnes Road. Three weekends of pool play, championship on the fourth. Public, ticketed, filmed, under the lights.

Officials, trainers, crew

A new sport needs referees who learn it before anyone else, medical staff on every game day, and a video crew. If that's you, get in touch.

Sponsors and vendors

The Invitational is looking for a presenting sponsor and for food trucks for each game day. Every player in the first footage, every ticket, and every stream carries the presenting sponsor.
