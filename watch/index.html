How to read a game

  • 6 Touchdown. Then watch the point-after: it starts instantly, and the scorer can dunk it for 3, reset for 3, or take the 1.
  • 3 Target. A throw through the ring on the crossbar, from anywhere, any time.
  • 1 Uprights. Thrown or kicked, any time.
  • No downs. When the ball changes hands, the game just turns around.
  • 80 minutes on a fixed clock. The scoreboard never lies about how long is left.
